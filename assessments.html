Assessment 1 — Presentation

40% · Individual or group oral presentation with Q&A · due Session 5

An oral assessment which consists of a verbal demonstration of learning based on specific research results identified on a topic area. The oral assessment is prepared outside of class hours and is part of independent learning and it uses appropriate concepts and theories from the course. A question-and-answer session may be included at the end of the presentation. Scope (Teacher's Notes): key concepts from Units 1 and 2, particularly the fundamentals of securities markets and blockchain, going beyond the class materials through additional research.

ILOs assessed: ILO 1 ILO 2

Assessment 2 — Report & Presentation

60% · Written report and oral presentation with Q&A · due Session 9

An oral assessment which consists of a verbal demonstration of learning based on specific research results identified on a topic area. The oral assessment is prepared outside of class hours and is part of independent learning and it uses appropriate concepts and theories from the course. The written assessment includes details of the above noted research. A question-and-answer session may be included at the end of the presentation. Scope (Teacher's Notes): the full course (Units 1–4), integrating traditional market structures, blockchain fundamentals and their intersection, with real-world references, case applications and conceptual analysis. Optional enhancement: propose an innovative blockchain-based solution or product, e.g. tokenising a traditional financial asset, a decentralised compliance system or a blockchain-powered cross-border payment model.

ILOs assessed: ILO 1 ILO 2 ILO 3 ILO 4 ILO 5
