Live Market Lab
Core message · Unit 1
A trade is only a promise until settlement; markets are worth what their plumbing guarantees, not what their screens display.
  • Students meet financial markets as prices on a screen, but every price rests on a chain of exchanges, clearinghouses, custodians and regulators.
  • The same chain, stretched across currencies, exposes firms to exchange-rate risk that parity theories explain only on average.
  • This unit trains students to follow a security and a currency from order to final settlement and to name where risk, cost and delay sit.
Intended learning outcomes addressed
2Analyze the market infrastructure, trading processes, and market participants involved in financial markets.
3Discuss the theoretical factors influencing foreign exchange rates, the roles of currency markets, and their consequences on international corporate finance, all within the context of blockchain technology.

Session question
What would it cost Isar Precision Tools to raise €50 million and protect its US dollar income, and who in the chain earns that cost?
1
Hook: one share, five institutions
15 min

Show a live quote for a DAX share on Yahoo Finance. Ask: if you bought it now, who holds it tonight and when is it legally yours? Collect answers on the board.

Output: List of guesses to revisit at the end

2
Mini-lecture: market architecture
25 min

Walk through the Securities Taxonomy, the Trade Lifecycle, CCP clearing and DvP using one equity and one bond trade.

Output: Annotated lifecycle on the board

3
Lab 1: read the market
35 min
  1. Teams pull price, bid-ask, volume and market cap for one equity and one government bond yield
  2. Compare spread and liquidity across a large-cap and a small-cap share
  3. Ask an AI tool to explain one term; verify it against the source

Output: One-slide market snapshot per team

Break · 15 minutes
4
Mini-lecture: FX determinants
20 min

PPP, interest rate parity, Fisher and the Impossible Trinity, applied to EUR/USD with current ECB and Federal Reserve policy rates.

5
Lab 2: price the currency risk
45 min
  1. Retrieve EUR/USD spot and the current ECB and Fed policy rates
  2. Calculate an indicative 12-month forward with covered interest parity
  3. Classify Isar's exposures as transaction, translation or economic
  4. Recommend a hedge for the transaction exposure

Output: Hedge recommendation with the forward calculation

6
Debrief and AI declaration
25 min

Teams present in 3 minutes each. Revisit the hook: who guarantees the trade? Each team declares its AI prompts and one AI error it caught.

Output: AI declaration line per team

Deliverable: A one-page memo to Isar's CFO: where the bond and share trades would clear and settle, the indicative forward rate, the exposure classification and one recommended hedge.

Materials

  • Laptops with Yahoo Finance and an approved AI tool
  • ECB and Federal Reserve websites for policy rates
  • Lifecycle and exposure templates (A3)
  • Calculator or spreadsheet

On-screen study only — copying, downloading and printing are disabled.
