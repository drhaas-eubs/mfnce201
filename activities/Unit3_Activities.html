Hackathon
Core message · Unit 3
Tokenisation changes the plumbing of finance, not the promise; a token is only as good as the legal claim and the cash leg behind it.
  • Banks, exchanges and DeFi protocols now issue bonds, fund units and derivatives as tokens that settle on shared ledgers.
  • The gains — atomic settlement, programmability, fractional access — appear only when the token is legally sound and paired with a reliable form of money on the same ledger.
  • This unit trains students to design a tokenised financial product end to end and to test it against regulation, settlement and DeFi mechanics.
Intended learning outcomes addressed
1Critically review the fundamental concepts and principles of blockchain technology and its potential applications in finance, foreign exchange, and securities markets.
3Discuss the theoretical factors influencing foreign exchange rates, the roles of currency markets, and their consequences on international corporate finance, all within the context of blockchain technology.
4Propose blockchain based solutions for improving market efficiency and transparency.

Session question
Can you design a tokenised investment product that a regulator would approve and an investor would buy?
1
Brief and team formation
15 min

Present the challenge: tokenise the Porta Nuova office building for retail and institutional investors under EU rules. Teams of 4–5 with defined roles: product, legal, technology, treasury, pitch.

2
Sprint 1: product and legal structure
35 min
  1. Choose the legal wrapper (fund unit, SPV bond, native DLT security)
  2. Classify the token with the Token Taxonomy and the Howey test
  3. Decide where it trades: DLT MTF, DLT SS or traditional venue

Output: Token term sheet draft

3
Sprint 2: settlement and cash leg
30 min
  1. Design DvP: stablecoin, tokenised deposit or central bank money link
  2. Decide how coupons or rents are paid in code
  3. Map the lifecycle on the Asset Tokenisation figure

Output: Settlement design

Break · 15 minutes
4
Sprint 3: DeFi and FX extension
30 min
  1. Could the token serve as DeFi collateral? At what ratio?
  2. How would a US or Swiss investor pay? Map the FX path
  3. Embed one compliance rule in the token (e.g. whitelist)

Output: Extension canvas

5
Pitches
45 min

Five-minute pitch per team to a panel (lecturer as regulator, two students as investors). Panel scores legal soundness, settlement design, investor value and risk awareness.

6
Debrief and AI declaration
10 min

What did tokenisation change, and what did it leave untouched? Each team states where it overruled the AI.

Deliverable: A token term sheet (2 pages): legal wrapper, classification, venue, settlement design, cash leg, coupon logic, one embedded compliance rule, key risks and an AI declaration.

Materials

  • Hackathon canvas (A2) per team
  • Laptops with an approved AI tool
  • Extracts of the DLT Pilot Regime and MiCA (Moodle)
  • Timer and scoring sheets

On-screen study only — copying, downloading and printing are disabled.
