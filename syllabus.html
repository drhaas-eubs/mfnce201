Course description

This course provides a comprehensive overview of securities, foreign exchange markets, and their integration with blockchain technology. Topics covered include the fundamentals of blockchain technology, the interplay of securities and foreign exchange markets, and their potential implications on the world of finance. The course also explores the use of blockchain technology in the securities and markets industry and the potential applications of blockchain-based solutions. By the end of this course, students will be able to explore the use of blockchain technology in the securities and markets industry and gain an understanding of the potential applications of blockchain-based solutions and their impact on financial services.

Intended learning outcomes

  1. Critically review the fundamental concepts and principles of blockchain technology and its potential applications in finance, foreign exchange, and securities markets.
  2. Analyze the market infrastructure, trading processes, and market participants involved in financial markets.
  3. Discuss the theoretical factors influencing foreign exchange rates, the roles of currency markets, and their consequences on international corporate finance, all within the context of blockchain technology.
  4. Propose blockchain based solutions for improving market efficiency and transparency.
  5. Critically assess the ethical considerations and social implications of blockchain adoption in finance, foreign exchange, and securities markets.
