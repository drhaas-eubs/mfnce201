Unit 1 · Securities, Markets and Foreign Exchange

HRSHans R. StollBid-Ask Spread Components

Why this thinker

Explained what liquidity providers are paid for when they quote a spread.

Intellectual foundation

Market microstructure theory and empirical analysis of dealer markets.

How to use the work

Decompose any spread into processing, inventory and information costs before comparing venues.

Limits and critique

Estimates depend on model assumptions and on the trading data available.

Impact

A standard reference in microstructure research and in venue-quality analysis.

GCGustav Cassel1866–1945Purchasing Power Parity

Why this thinker

Gave the long-run anchor for exchange rates its modern name.

Intellectual foundation

Swedish economist writing on the currency disorders after the First World War.

How to use the work

Compare relative inflation to judge whether a currency is over- or undervalued in the long run.

Limits and critique

Poor short-run predictor; trade costs and non-traded goods break the parity.

Impact

PPP underlies international price comparisons and long-term FX valuation.

JMKJohn Maynard Keynes1883–1946Interest Rate Parity

Why this thinker

Described the arbitrage link between interest rates and forward exchange rates.

Intellectual foundation

A Tract on Monetary Reform (1923), written amid post-war currency instability.

How to use the work

Price FX forwards from spot and interest differentials; test carry strategies.

Limits and critique

Uncovered parity often fails empirically, especially in the short run.

Impact

Covered interest parity is the basis of FX forward pricing in every bank treasury.

IFIrving Fisher1867–1947International Fisher Effect

Why this thinker

Separated nominal interest rates into real rates and expected inflation.

Intellectual foundation

The Theory of Interest (1930) and earlier work on money and prices.

How to use the work

Read interest-rate differentials as signals of inflation expectations between countries.

Limits and critique

The international extension has weak empirical support over short horizons.

Impact

The Fisher relation is standard in monetary economics and central banking.

RAMRobert A. Mundell1932–2021Impossible Trinity

Why this thinker

Showed why currency regimes force a choice between three policy goals.

Intellectual foundation

Open-economy macroeconomics; Nobel Memorial Prize in Economic Sciences 1999.

How to use the work

Analyse any currency regime by asking which of the three goals it gives up.

Limits and critique

A simplification; intermediate regimes and capital controls blur the corners.

Impact

Intellectual foundation of the euro debate and of optimal currency area theory.

Unit 2 · Introduction to Blockchain Technology

LLLeslie Lamportb. 1941Byzantine Generals Problem

Why this thinker

Formalised the problem every blockchain has to solve.

Intellectual foundation

Distributed systems research; Turing Award 2013.

How to use the work

Ask how many faulty or malicious nodes a system can tolerate before agreement fails.

Limits and critique

The original setting assumes known participants, unlike open blockchains.

Impact

BFT protocols derived from this work run many permissioned ledgers.

WDMWhitfield Diffie and Martin E. Hellmanb. 1944 · b. 1945Public-Key Cryptography

Why this thinker

Made it possible to prove ownership and authorisation without sharing a secret.

Intellectual foundation

Cryptography research at Stanford; Turing Award 2015.

How to use the work

Explain wallets, signatures and custody as problems of key control.

Limits and critique

Security depends entirely on keeping the private key safe.

Impact

Public-key cryptography secures the internet and every blockchain.

SNSatoshi NakamotoHash-Linked Chain of Blocks

Why this thinker

Combined existing ideas into the first working ledger without a central keeper.

Intellectual foundation

The 2008 Bitcoin white paper; the author's identity remains unknown.

How to use the work

Read the white paper as the reference design for proof-of-work blockchains.

Limits and critique

Proof of work is slow and energy-intensive; governance is informal.

Impact

Started the field of cryptocurrencies and distributed ledger finance.

DDDaniel DrescherBlockchain Types Matrix

Why this thinker

Explained blockchain to non-technical readers in a structured way.

Intellectual foundation

Blockchain Basics (2017), on the course's recommended reading list.

How to use the work

Use the read/write distinction to classify any ledger quickly.

Limits and critique

Introductory; does not cover later developments such as proof of stake in depth.

Impact

Widely used as an entry text in business and finance courses.

NSNick SzaboSmart Contracts

Why this thinker

Coined and defined the idea of smart contracts long before blockchains existed.

Intellectual foundation

Computer science and law; essays in the 1990s on digital contracts.

How to use the work

Ask which clauses of a contract can be executed by code and which need judgement.

Limits and critique

Real contracts rely on interpretation that code cannot provide.

Impact

His concept became the basis of Ethereum and DeFi.

VBVitalik Buterinb. 1994Ethereum as a World Computer · Blockchain Trilemma

Why this thinker

Turned blockchain from a payment ledger into a programmable platform.

Intellectual foundation

Co-founder of Ethereum; white paper published in 2014.

How to use the work

Understand DeFi, tokens and dApps as smart contracts on a general-purpose chain.

Limits and critique

Scalability and governance remain open problems he himself discusses.

Impact

Ethereum hosts the largest share of DeFi and tokenisation activity.

KWAKarl Wüst and Arthur GervaisDo You Need a Blockchain?

Why this thinker

Gave decision-makers a short, rigorous test against blockchain hype.

Intellectual foundation

Security research at ETH Zurich and Imperial College London.

How to use the work

Run every blockchain proposal through the decision tree before any design work.

Limits and critique

Binary questions simplify trust, which is often partial in practice.

Impact

Frequently cited in business, policy and engineering discussions of blockchain.

MIKMarco Iansiti and Karim R. LakhaniBlockchain Adoption Matrix

Why this thinker

Framed blockchain as foundational, not disruptive, and slowed the hype.

Intellectual foundation

Harvard Business School research on digital transformation and innovation.

How to use the work

Position a use case by novelty and coordination to estimate its adoption path.

Limits and critique

Analogy with TCP/IP may not hold for regulated financial markets.

Impact

One of the most cited management articles on blockchain; on the course reading list.

Unit 3 · Application of Blockchain Technology in Finance

RARaphael AuerEmbedded Supervision

Why this thinker

Showed how regulation could be built into the ledger itself.

Intellectual foundation

Economist at the Bank for International Settlements.

How to use the work

Design compliance rules into tokens and plan supervisory access from the start.

Limits and critique

Depends on economic finality, which public chains offer only probabilistically.

Impact

Influential in central bank and supervisor thinking on DLT markets.

FSFabian SchärDeFi Stack

Why this thinker

Provided the first widely used structured map of decentralised finance.

Intellectual foundation

Research on distributed ledger technology and fintech at the University of Basel.

How to use the work

Locate any DeFi product in the stack and trace its dependencies.

Limits and critique

A snapshot of a fast-moving field; later protocols add new layers.

Impact

Standard academic reference for DeFi in finance teaching.

HAHayden AdamsConstant Product Market Maker

Why this thinker

Built Uniswap, which made automated market making a mainstream DeFi primitive.

Intellectual foundation

Uniswap protocol white papers (v1–v3).

How to use the work

Explain price formation and slippage in liquidity pools with x · y = k.

Limits and critique

Liquidity providers face impermanent loss; pools can be manipulated.

Impact

AMMs became the dominant form of decentralised exchange.

RJSRobert J. Shillerb. 1946Perpetual Futures

Why this thinker

Proposed perpetual claims for assets that lack liquid markets.

Intellectual foundation

Financial economics; Nobel Memorial Prize in Economic Sciences 2013.

How to use the work

Explain how funding payments replace expiry in crypto perpetual futures.

Limits and critique

His proposal targeted real estate indices, not leveraged crypto trading.

Impact

Perpetual futures are now the most traded crypto derivative.

EFFEugene F. Famab. 1939Efficient Market Hypothesis

Why this thinker

Defined what it means for prices to reflect information.

Intellectual foundation

Empirical finance at the University of Chicago; Nobel Memorial Prize 2013.

How to use the work

Test whether blockchain transparency changes how quickly information reaches prices.

Limits and critique

Behavioural finance documents persistent anomalies.

Impact

Foundation of index investing and of modern market-efficiency research.

LLLawrence Lessigb. 1961Lessig's Four Modalities

Why this thinker

Showed that code regulates behaviour as powerfully as law.

Intellectual foundation

Constitutional and cyber law at Harvard Law School.

How to use the work

Sort every governance proposal into law, norms, market and architecture.

Limits and critique

Written before blockchains; does not address decentralised code authorship directly.

Impact

'Code is law' became a defining phrase of the blockchain debate.

BFBatya FriedmanValue-Sensitive Design

Why this thinker

Made human values a design requirement, not an afterthought.

Intellectual foundation

Human-computer interaction research at the University of Washington.

How to use the work

List values and indirect stakeholders before choosing a technical architecture.

Limits and critique

Identifying values does not resolve conflicts between them.

Impact

Widely used in technology ethics and responsible innovation.

JEJohn Elkingtonb. 1949Triple Bottom Line

Why this thinker

Extended performance measurement beyond profit.

Intellectual foundation

Sustainability consulting; Cannibals with Forks (1997).

How to use the work

Assess blockchain solutions for social and environmental as well as financial effects.

Limits and critique

Elkington himself called for the concept to be rethought in 2018.

Impact

Shaped sustainability reporting and ESG practice.

JFMJackie Fenn and Mark RaskinoHype Cycle

Why this thinker

Gave managers a vocabulary for the rise and fall of technology expectations.

Intellectual foundation

Technology research at Gartner.

How to use the work

Time adoption decisions by placing a technology on the curve.

Limits and critique

Descriptive model; not all technologies follow the curve.

Impact

Used widely in strategy and innovation management.
