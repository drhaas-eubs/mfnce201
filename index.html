This is the working environment for MFNCE201 in the Master in Finance. The course moves from how securities and currency markets work today, through the fundamentals of blockchain, to tokenised assets, DeFi and the regulatory, ethical and strategic consequences for finance. Each unit opens with a core message, two Slibraries of established frameworks, a composite teaching case, a pre-reading, a session plan and a reflection prompt. AI runs through the course as a working tool and a topic: AI drafts, the student decides, every AI contribution is declared.

Module profile

Unit of analysisThe trade: from order to final settlement
